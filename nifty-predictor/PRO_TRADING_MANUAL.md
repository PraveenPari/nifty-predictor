## 📈 UNDERSTANDING THE NUMBERS

### Risk-Reward Ratio (R:R)
```
Good: 1.5:1 or higher
Best: 2:1 or higher
Excellent: 3:1

Formula: (Target - Entry) / (Entry - SL)
Example: (24625 - 24550) / (24550 - 24512) = 75 / 38 = 1.97:1
```

### Lot Size Calculation
```
Formula: Risk Amount / (Points Risk × 75)

Example:
- Capital: ₹100,000
- Risk: 2% = ₹2,000
- Points Risk: 38.10
- Lots: 2000 / (38.10 × 75) = 0.7 → Round to 1 lot
```

### Profit Calculation
```
Formula: Points Profit × Lots × 75

Example:
- Entry: 24,550
- Exit: 24,625
- Points: 75
- Lots: 2
- Profit: 75 × 2 × 75 = ₹11,250
- Percentage: (75 / 24,550) × 100 = 0.31%
```

## 🔍 READING THE SIGNALS

### RSI (14)
- Below 30 = Oversold (BUY)
- Above 70 = Overbought (SELL)
- 30-70 = Neutral zone

### MACD
- Green bars = Bullish (BUY)
- Red bars = Bearish (SELL)
- Crossovers = Trend change

### Moving Averages
- Fast above Slow = Bullish (Golden Cross)
- Fast below Slow = Bearish (Death Cross)

### Bollinger Bands
- Price at lower band = Oversold (BUY)
- Price at upper band = Overbought (SELL)
- Squeeze = Breakout coming

### ADX
- Above 25 = Strong trend (Trade the trend)
- Below 25 = Weak trend (Range trade)
- Rising = Trend strengthening
- Falling = Trend weakening

### SuperTrend
- Green line = Support (BUY at green line)
- Red line = Resistance (SELL at red line)
- Color flip = Trend reversal
